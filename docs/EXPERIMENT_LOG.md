# Experiment log

What was run, when, and what came of it — newest first. One row per **campaign**,
not per cell: a campaign is a question we set out to answer and the batch of runs
that answered it.

- What each arm *is*, and what it needs to run: [EXPERIMENTS.md](EXPERIMENTS.md)
- Current numbers, regenerated by `baseline.eval`: [BASELINE_RESULTS.md](BASELINE_RESULTS.md)
- Per-cell finish dates: the Coverage section of BASELINE_RESULTS.md

## About the dates

Two kinds, and they are not interchangeable.

**Recorded** — from 2026-08-19 onward, every finished prediction run writes a
`.meta.json` sidecar next to its result CSV carrying `started_utc`,
`finished_utc` and the commit it ran at. Those are real run times, and
`baseline.eval` reads them into the Coverage section.

**Commit-dated** — everything before that. The result CSVs are gitignored and
every file timestamp in `data/annotated/baseline/` and `outputs/baseline_eval/`
is `2026-08-16`, the date of a bulk rescore rather than of any run. So there is
no per-cell run date to recover, and inferring one from a mtime would attach a
confident wrong date to a real result. The dates below for those campaigns are
the dates of the commits that landed them: a lower bound on when the work ran,
which is the strongest honest claim available.

Mixed cases: the retrieval/agentic rows are **recorded** (their cells carry
`.meta.json` sidecars). The classic, synthesis and self-training rows are
**inferred** from source/script mtimes and SLURM job logs, because those
pathways write outside `data/annotated/baseline/` and so produce no sidecar.
Those four campaigns were reconstructed on 2026-09-08 from artifacts on disk,
having never been logged at the time.

## Adding a campaign

1. Create `docs/experiments/YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md` — copy the structure of the
   teacher-forcing entry: question, why now, what changed, how to run, cells
   produced, results, verdict.
2. Add a row here, at the top.
3. Leave the numbers to `baseline.eval`. A campaign doc quotes the figures it
   argues from and links out for the rest; it never becomes a second place where
   results are maintained by hand.
